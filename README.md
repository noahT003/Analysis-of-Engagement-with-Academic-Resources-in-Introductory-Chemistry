# Analysis of Engagement with Academic Resources in Introductory Chemistry

## Overview
My university's chemistry class has students take a quantitative test that assesses their skills in using numbers in math and science. This test has shown to be very predictive of a student's final grade in the course. Multiple opportunities for engagement have been introduced for students' benefit, and we seek to investigate the effectiveness of these resources.

## Objective
Does engagement with academic resources help make a meaningful difference in students' grades overall and for those who scored low on the quantitative assessment?

## Data
Anonymized students who took my university's introductory chemistry class and consented to have data taken of them. Lists exam results, engagement, and other metrics of their performance in class. There is no demographic information about the student, and the only data that exists of them pertains to their performance in this chemistry class.

## Tools Used
R. Utilized ggplot2, tidyverse and lme4 packages.
